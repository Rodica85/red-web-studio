# RED Web Studio — Website Packages & Pricing (v2, accessible)

Replaces v1 and the old table in [business-plan.md](business-plan.md). GBP, excl. VAT.
Hosting and domain are paid by the client directly (about £60-120/year).

## Why these prices
- Direct peers sell a one-page site at £299 and five pages at ~£800, with monthly payment.
  Freelancers on the wider UK market charge £1,500-3,000 for five pages.
- Target: clearly cheaper than the freelancer market, level with the cheapest studios on
  the entry package, with real custom design and live proof behind it.
- Local small businesses (massage, trades, mobile services) decide on monthly cost, so every
  package can be paid monthly.

## Add-ons
Extra page £60 · Logo £149 · Copywriting £40 per page · Social media starter pack £99.

## Terms
50% deposit, 50% on launch. Client provides photos and text, or buys the copywriting add-on.
30 days of free fixes after launch. Client owns domain, content and design files.

## Rules
- Business (£790) is the package to sell. Starter is the entry door, one round of edits only.
- If a client asks for a discount, reduce scope (fewer pages), never the price.
- Business is about 20 hours of work and Growth about 40, so prices below these drop under £30/hour.
