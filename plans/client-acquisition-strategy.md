# RED Web Studio — Client Acquisition Strategy (Nov 2026)

## Starting point (honest)
- Solo studio, 7 live client sites, very few testimonials, small budget.
- Direct competitors at the low end (e.g. Raduvis Studio: £299 one-page, £800 five-page,
  monthly payment option) already win on price. Competing on price means losing.
- Best proof is in local service businesses: wellness (Gung Chaba), mobile services
  (Luca Mobile Sauna), logistics and local SEO (Red Speed Rider), healthcare (Dr. Stan).

## Goal
- 30 days: 2-3 paying clients (first ones with testimonial-for-discount).
- 90 days: 1 new client every 2 weeks, 3+ care plan subscribers, 5+ testimonials.

## 1. Positioning: stop being "a web designer"
**"Websites that get local service businesses booked."**
Niche: independent local service businesses (wellness, trades, transport, mobile services,
clinics, events). Pitch outcome: more bookings from Google and Facebook, not "a nice site".
Every ad, post, profile and DM says the same thing.

## 2. Offer
- Entry offer is the **Free Audit** (already live): a real score on their own site.
- Packages: see [pricing-packages.md](pricing-packages.md). Add monthly payment option
  (e.g. spread over 12 months) to lower the buying barrier.
- Care plan on every project, recurring revenue is what makes the studio stable.

## 3. Channels, ranked by return on effort

| # | Channel | Cost | Time to first result | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Existing clients: referral + testimonial | £0 | 1-2 weeks | Fastest warm leads and proof |
| 2 | Partners who serve the same clients (photographers, accountants, branding/print, marketing freelancers, business coaches, estate agents) | £0 | 2-4 weeks | Recurring referral source, they meet the client first |
| 3 | Outbound to a defined list (audit-led) | £0 | 2-4 weeks | Volume, controllable daily |
| 4 | Google Business Profile + own-site local SEO | £0 | 2-3 months | Inbound that compounds |
| 5 | Facebook groups + portfolio posts | £0 | 1-2 months | Authority, warm inbound |
| 6 | Directories and showcases (niche-specific) | £0 | 1-3 months | Backlinks, passive leads |
| 7 | Facebook / Instagram ads, lead form with audit offer | £150-300/mo | After 3 testimonials | Scale what already works |

Do 1-3 now, set up 4-6 in parallel (one-off setup), turn on 7 only when proof exists.

## 4. The funnel (what happens after first contact)
1. **Hook:** Free Audit result, or a mention from a referrer.
2. **Conversation:** 10-15 minute call or chat, understand their goal (more bookings, calls, quotes).
3. **Proof:** send 2 relevant live sites + a homepage sketch.
4. **Proposal:** one page, one recommended package, fixed price, monthly option.
5. **Close:** 50% deposit, kickoff within 3 days.
6. **After launch:** testimonial request at day 14, referral ask at day 30, care plan offer at launch.

## 5. Weekly operating rhythm (about 1-1.5 hours per day)
- **Mon:** Build/refresh the prospect list (20 new, with their audit score). 
- **Tue-Thu:** Outreach block (5-10 contacts/day), follow-ups, calls.
- **Fri:** One portfolio post, one partner conversation, review numbers.
- **Monthly:** Ask each client for a referral, ask one finished client for a testimonial.

## 6. Numbers to track (weekly)
Contacts made → replies → calls → proposals → closed.
Planning ratio: ~200 contacts → ~15 replies → ~6 calls → 2-3 clients.
If replies are under 5%, the problem is the list or hook, not volume. If calls convert under
25%, the problem is price or proof.

## 7. 90-day plan

**Weeks 1-2: Foundation**
- Google Business Profile live, with all 7 projects.
- Ask all existing clients for testimonial + referral.
- Choose the niche + area, build list of 100 prospects with audit scores.
- List 10 partners to approach.
- Fix pricing on website and in ads copy to match [pricing-packages.md](pricing-packages.md).

**Weeks 3-6: Volume**
- Daily outreach to the list, weekly partner conversations.
- Weekly portfolio post + join 3-5 local business groups and contribute.
- Close first 2-3 clients (discount for testimonial).

**Weeks 7-10: Proof**
- Publish testimonials and mini case studies (before/after score, bookings).
- Directory listings.
- Add care plans to every finished project.

**Weeks 11-13: Scale**
- Start Facebook lead-form ads at £5-10/day with Free Audit as the offer.
- Double down on whichever channel produced the most closed clients.
- Raise prices if closing rate is above 30%.

## 8. What not to do
- Don't chase price-only buyers (the £300 crowd).
- Don't spend on ads before proof exists.
- Don't spread across 6 niches.
- Don't skip the care plan: one-off projects keep the studio at zero every month.

## Open decisions
- Target area: local (Oxfordshire / Abingdon area, where the portfolio is) or Romania or both.
- Hours per day available.
- Whether to add the monthly payment option now.
