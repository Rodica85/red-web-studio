How Much Does a Small Business Website Cost in the UK?

A plain guide to what people really pay, what changes the price and what to ask before you hire a web designer.

Let's Talk → See Pricing

The short answer: a small business website in the UK typically costs between a few hundred pounds for a one-page site and a few thousand for a multi-page custom site, depending on who builds it. At RED Web Studio, prices are fixed and one-time: £349 for one page, £790 for up to five pages and £1,290 for up to ten pages.

Ranges for freelancers and agencies come from published UK pricing guides, such as those from Whito and ProfileTree. Real quotes vary with the size of the site, the content and the features.

What changes the price

  • ✓ Number of pages. One page is a different job from ten.
  • ✓ Custom design or a template. A design built around your business takes longer than a pre-made one.
  • ✓ Features. Booking, payments, a shop, more than one language.
  • ✓ Text and photos. Who writes the copy and who supplies the images.
  • ✓ SEO. Whether the site is set up to be found on Google from day one.

The costs people forget

Ask any designer what is not in the quote. Common extras are the domain name, hosting, maintenance and edits after launch. With RED Web Studio, a domain costs about £10 to £15 a year and hosting is typically free to around £60 a year, paid directly to the provider with no markup. Maintenance is optional and month-to-month, from £25 a month.

Questions to ask before you hire anyone

  • ✓ Is the price fixed, and what exactly does it include?
  • ✓ Will I own the domain, the content and the design files?
  • ✓ How many rounds of changes are included?
  • ✓ Is there a monthly fee, and can I stop it?
  • ✓ Can I see sites you have built and speak to the person who will build mine?

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a small business website cost in the UK?+

It depends on who builds it. Published UK guides report about £1,500 to £3,000 for a five-page site from a freelancer and £3,000 to £5,000 from a regional agency. RED Web Studio charges a fixed £349 for one page, £790 for up to five pages and £1,290 for up to ten.

Is a cheap website a bad sign?+

Not always, but a very low price for a complete business website is worth questioning. Ask what is included: design, mobile layout, SEO, your own domain and who owns the site.

Do I have to pay monthly for a website?+

Not with RED Web Studio. The price is one-time. Maintenance is optional and month-to-month, from £25 a month, and covers updates, backups and security.
