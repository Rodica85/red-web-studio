Privacy Policy

What personal information RED Web Studio receives, why, and what your rights are. Written to be read, not skimmed past.

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Who we are

RED Web Studio is a web design studio based in the United Kingdom. It is a sole-trader business run by Rodica, who is the data controller for anything in this policy: the person who decides how your information is used.

The owner's full name and a postal address are available on request.

Contact: contact@redwebstudio.com or +44 7424 714686.

The short version

  • This website has no database and no customer accounts: it stores nothing about you.
  • We only receive the details you choose to send us, by WhatsApp, email or phone.
  • This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers.
  • The chat assistant does not store your conversation.
  • We never sell your information or use it for anyone else's marketing.

What we receive, and why

When you get in touch

If you contact us by the contact form, WhatsApp, email or phone, we receive what you tell us: usually your name, email address, phone number, business name and your message.

The contact form does not send your details to our website. It opens WhatsApp with your message ready, and you send it from your own WhatsApp account.

We use these details to reply, to prepare a quote and to discuss your project. Our lawful basis is taking steps at your request before a contract, and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries.

When you become a client

We keep the contact details, content and files you give us for your project, plus invoices and payment records. We need these to deliver the work (contract) and to meet tax and accounting rules (legal obligation).

When you use the chat assistant

The chat on this website is an AI assistant that answers questions about our services. Your message is sent to Cloudflare, which runs the AI model that writes the reply.

  • We do not store your conversation, and it is gone when you close the page.
  • Cloudflare states that it does not keep the messages after replying and does not use them to train AI models.
  • If you type an email address or phone number into the chat, that message is not sent to the AI. The assistant asks you to use the "Start a project" button instead, which opens WhatsApp.
  • The assistant can make mistakes. Prices and terms are confirmed in writing by us, not by the chat.

Please do not type sensitive personal information into the chat.

When you visit the website

Like every website, ours is delivered by service providers that briefly process technical data such as your IP address and browser type, in order to show you the pages and keep the site secure:

  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, so your browser connects to Google when a page loads.
  • Cloudflare delivers the chat assistant.

We do not use this data to identify you or to follow you around the internet.

Cookies

This website does not set cookies, and it has no analytics, advertising or social media tracking. That is why there is no cookie banner.

Who we share information with

Only with the services needed to run the business and this website: WhatsApp (Meta) and our email provider for messages, Vercel for hosting, Cloudflare for the chat assistant and Google for fonts. Some of these companies are based outside the UK and process data under their own terms, including safeguards for international transfers.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone for marketing.

How long we keep it

We do not build or keep a customer database. The only personal information we hold is the messages you send us and, if you become a client, what is needed for your project and for our accounts.

  • Enquiries that do not become a project: deleted within 3 months.
  • Client project files and messages: kept while we work together and for as long as you may need support.
  • Invoices and payment records: kept for as long as UK tax and accounting rules require.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your information, or stop or limit how we use it;
  • send your information to you or to another provider.

To do any of these, email contact@redwebstudio.com. We will reply within one month, free of charge.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
