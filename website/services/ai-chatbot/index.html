How It Actually Works

Before we write a line of chatbot configuration, we sit down and document your business properly: services, pricing structure, policies, opening hours, the questions your customers ask most often. That becomes the chatbot's knowledge base. When a visitor asks something within that scope, it answers directly and accurately. When they ask something outside it (a specific medical question, a legal opinion, anything the chatbot genuinely shouldn't guess at), it says so plainly and offers to get a human to follow up, capturing their contact details as a lead in the process.

That escalation behaviour isn't an afterthought. A chatbot that confidently makes things up when it doesn't know an answer causes real problems for a business: wrong prices quoted, wrong medical or legal information implied, customers turning up expecting something you don't offer. We'd rather it says "let me get someone to help you with that" than guess.

Built With Privacy In Mind, Not As An Afterthought

RED Web Studio's approach to chatbot design grew out of academic research into ethical AI chatbot design and privacy-by-design principles, not just a plugin bolted onto a website. In practice, that means conversation data is stored with UK/EU-based providers, your customers' conversations are never used to train external AI models, and you get a privacy policy template that properly covers chatbot interactions instead of leaving a compliance gap. GDPR isn't treated as paperwork to get through after launch. It shapes how the chatbot is built from the start.

It Improves With Every Conversation

Launch isn't the end of the process. You get access to a log of the questions visitors actually ask, which is often more revealing than any survey: you'll see exactly where your current pricing page confuses people, which service gets asked about most, and where the chatbot needed to hand off to a human. That feedback loop lets you (or us, if you'd rather) tighten up its answers over time, so it gets more accurate and more useful the longer it runs, instead of staying frozen at launch-day quality.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the AI chatbot GDPR compliant?+

Yes. We use UK/EU-based AI providers and store all conversation data in compliance with GDPR. We provide a privacy policy template covering chatbot interactions, and we never train external models on your customer data.

What happens if the chatbot doesn't know the answer?+

It politely says it will get a human to follow up and captures the visitor's contact details as a lead, instead of guessing or making something up.

Can the chatbot book appointments?+

Yes, depending on your booking system, the chatbot can guide visitors to book directly or collect their details and preferred time for you to confirm.

Does the chatbot open automatically and annoy visitors?+

No. It stays closed until a visitor chooses to open it themselves. We design chatbot behaviour to be helpful when wanted, not intrusive.
