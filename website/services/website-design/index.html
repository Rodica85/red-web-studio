Built For Your Business, Not A Template

Every project starts with a conversation, not a template picker. We talk about what your customers actually need to see: opening hours, pricing, booking, photos of your work, reviews, and design the site around that, in your brand's own voice and colours. That's the difference between a site that looks like it was made in an afternoon and one that makes a stranger trust you enough to get in touch. Because we hand-code every site rather than assembling it from a drag-and-drop builder, we can make the exact layout your business needs, instead of bending your business to fit whatever a template allows.

This also means your site stays lean. No unused plugin code, no bloated builder framework loading in the background, just the HTML, CSS, and JavaScript your pages actually need. That directly affects two things Google and your visitors both care about: page speed and mobile usability. A site that loads in under two seconds keeps visitors around long enough to read your offer, instead of bouncing back to the search results after five.

Performance And Technical Foundations

Design is only half the job. Underneath the visuals, every RED site is built on clean, semantic HTML with structured data markup, so search engines and AI assistants like ChatGPT and Google's AI Overviews can accurately understand what your business does, where it operates, and how to contact you. Images are compressed and lazy-loaded, fonts are preloaded, and unnecessary scripts are stripped out, which keeps Core Web Vitals (the speed metrics Google uses to rank sites) comfortably in the "good" range. We also set up basic security hygiene as standard: HTTPS, safe external links, and no exposed form endpoints that could be abused for spam.

None of this is visible to a visitor scrolling your homepage, but it's the difference between a site that ranks and loads well for years, and one that quietly slows down and drops off page one within a few months.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a custom website design take?+

Most one-page sites launch in about 5 days from kickoff. Multi-page sites typically take about 7 days. The clock starts once we've had the kickoff call and received your content.

Do you use WordPress, Wix, or Squarespace?+

No. Every RED Web Studio site is hand-coded with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. This means faster load times, no plugin bloat, no monthly platform fees, and no vendor lock-in. You own the code outright.

Will my website work on mobile phones?+

Yes. Every site is designed mobile-first, meaning we design and test for phones before scaling up to tablet and desktop. Most visitors to independent business websites arrive on mobile, so this is non-negotiable.

Can I update the website content myself after launch?+

Yes, every site comes with a simple content editor, a 30-minute walkthrough video, and a written guide, so you can edit text, swap images, and add content without writing code.
