About us

From our grill
to your plate

Fire & Feast was founded by our head chef with one simple idea: real food, cooked over real fire, in front of your guests. Every platter is prepared to order. We don't keep stock, so the meat is always fresh, marinated the day before and grilled on the spot.

Our guests' favourites: suckling pig on the rotisserie with crispy crackling, juicy skewers, and grilled peppers, courgettes and potatoes straight from the fire.

From a small family gathering to a big wedding, we take care of everything: delivery, set-up, cooking and serving. You just enjoy your day.
