About

Groomed with patience

Hi, I'm the groomer behind Pawfect. I set up a one-to-one studio because nervous and older dogs deserve a calmer experience than a busy salon.

Every dog gets my full attention, gentle handling and as many treats as their owner allows.

  • Cage-free: your dog is never shut in a crate
  • Great with nervous, senior and rescue dogs
  • Natural, hypoallergenic shampoos
  • Fully insured and first-aid trained
