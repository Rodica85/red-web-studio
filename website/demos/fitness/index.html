FAQ

Good questions

I'm a complete beginner. Is that OK?

Absolutely. Most of my clients start from zero. We begin with the basics and build up at your pace.

Where do sessions take place?

At a private studio in Nottingham city centre, outdoors in the park, or online from your living room.

Do I have to follow a strict diet?

No. You'll get simple, flexible nutrition guidance that fits the food you already enjoy.

Can I cancel or pause?

Yes. Packs don't expire for 6 months and online coaching is month-to-month.
