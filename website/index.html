Frequently Asked Questions

How does payment work?+

Each package has one fixed price. You pay 50% to start and 50% on launch, with no instalments and no hidden fees.

Do I need a maintenance plan?+

No, it's optional and month-to-month. Your site belongs to you and works without it. Plans start at £25/month and cover updates, backups and security, with hours for edits on Plus and Pro. One-off changes are quoted before we start.

What if I'm not happy with the design?+

Every package includes design revisions: 1 round on Starter, 2 on Business and 3 on Growth, plus 30 days of free fixes after launch. The deposit is refundable any time before you approve the design.

What does the site cost to run each year?+

A domain costs about £10-£15 a year. Hosting is typically free to about £60 a year. You pay the providers directly, with no markup from us.

Who owns the website after delivery?+

You do. You own the code, content, domain and design files, and you can move to another provider at any time.

Is a customer chat included?+

Yes, a simple customer-service chat is included in Business and Growth. Smarter AI-powered versions are quoted per project.

Do you do local SEO?+

Yes, on every site: Google Business Profile text, local schema and keywords for your area. Rankings take time, so we show you before-and-after speed and SEO scores.

How long does it take?+

Starter about 5 days, Business 7-10 days, Growth 2-3 weeks. We count from the kickoff call and once we have your content.
