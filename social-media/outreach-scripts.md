# RED Web Studio — Outreach Scripts (using the Free Audit tool)

Goal: turn the live PageSpeed Free Audit on the site into a lead-generation hook.
Instead of a generic "do you need a website?" DM, lead with a real, specific finding
about *their* current site. Specific > generic, every time.

How to use this doc:
1. Pick 5-10 prospects/day from the lists below.
2. Run their URL through the Free Audit on https://www.redwebstudio.com (or PageSpeed Insights
   directly if they have no site at all — then use the "no website" script instead).
3. Copy the matching template, swap in the bracketed details, personalise one line.
4. Send it yourself from your own FB/Instagram/email/LinkedIn account — sending on your
   behalf isn't something I can do, but the copy is ready to paste.

---

## Where to find prospects

- **Google Maps**: search "[service] in [town]" — anyone with no website link, or a
  Facebook page as their only "website", is a prospect.
- **Facebook Business groups you're already targeting** (see [strategy.md](facebook/strategy.md))
  — watch for "does anyone know a good web designer?" threads.
- **Directories relevant to your real niches**: massage/wellness directories, man-and-van
  / logistics directories, medical practice directories, indie app/SaaS showcases
  (Product Hunt, BetaList, IndieHackers) — mirrors your actual portfolio (Gung Chaba,
  Red Speed Rider, Dr. Stan Larisa, LolyTales).

## Template A — They have a website, but it scores badly

> Subject/opener: Quick thing I noticed about [Business Name]'s website
>
> Hi [Name], I run RED Web Studio — we build custom websites for independent
> businesses. I ran a free speed/SEO check on [businessname.com] out of curiosity and
> it's currently scoring [X]/100 on Google's PageSpeed test, mainly because of [top
> issue, e.g. "slow image loading" / "no mobile optimisation" / "missing meta
> descriptions"]. That's likely costing you visitors before they even see your page.
>
> No pitch attached — you can run the same free check yourself here:
> https://www.redwebstudio.com/#contact (Free Audit section). If you ever want a hand
> fixing it, happy to chat, no pressure either way.

## Template B — No website at all

> Hi [Name], I noticed [Business Name] doesn't have a website yet (just the Facebook/
> Instagram page) — you clearly have real customers already based on your reviews, so
> a proper site would likely convert even more of them, especially anyone finding you
> through Google search.
>
> I build custom sites for independent businesses like [comparable portfolio piece —
> e.g. "Gung Chaba, a massage studio in Abingdon" if local service, or "an indie app
> called LolyTales" if digital product]. Would you be open to a free, no-obligation
> chat about what a simple site could look like for you?

## Template C — Warm intro after a Facebook group interaction

> Thanks for the reply earlier — following up on what you asked about [topic]. If it's
> useful, here's a quick free audit tool I built that checks any site's speed/SEO in
> a few seconds: https://www.redwebstudio.com/#contact. No strings attached, just thought
> it might help since you mentioned [their pain point].

## Template D — Referral-style intro (for warm leads via existing clients)

> Hi [Name], [existing client]'s recommended I reach out — I built their site at
> [gungchaba.online / redspeedrider.co.uk / doctorstanlarisa.ro / lolytales.com].
> Happy to send over a couple of examples and a free audit of your current site if
> useful, no pressure.

## Follow-up cadence (if no reply)

- Day 0: initial message (template above)
- Day 4: soft bump — "No worries if now's not the right time — just wanted to bump
  this in case it got buried!"
- Day 14: value bump — send one specific, useful tip unprompted (no ask), e.g. "saw
  your site still isn't showing on Google Maps search — happy to point you to a free
  fix if useful." Then let it go.

Never more than 2 follow-ups. Groups/local reputation matter more than pushing.

---

## Daily target

5-10 personalised outreach messages/day, 5 days/week = 25-50/week. At a realistic
5-10% reply rate and 20-30% of replies converting, that's roughly 1-2 new
conversations → 1 client every 1-2 weeks from this channel alone, on top of referrals
and inbound from the site itself.
