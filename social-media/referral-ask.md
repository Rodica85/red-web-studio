# RED Web Studio — Referral & Testimonial Ask (existing clients)

You have 4 live, real clients already. This is the highest-conversion channel
available and it's currently untapped. Send these yourself (WhatsApp/email — whichever
you already use with each client); I can't message them on your behalf.

Two asks, kept separate so it doesn't feel transactional:
1. A short testimonial/review (use for site + social proof)
2. A referral (£50 incentive, per the business plan)

---

## 1. Testimonial request (send first, ~2-4 weeks after launch when they've used the site)

> Hi [Name], hope the new site's been working well for you! Quick favour — would you
> mind sending me 2-3 sentences about your experience working together (what it was
> like, and any results you've noticed, e.g. more bookings/enquiries)? I'd love to add
> it to the site and share it on social media, with a link back to
> [gungchaba.online / redspeedrider.co.uk / doctorstanlarisa.ro / lolytales.com]
> obviously. No pressure at all if you're busy — just let me know either way.

If they say yes but go quiet, one follow-up after a week is fine, then drop it.

---

## 2. Referral ask (send once testimonial is in, or if they're clearly happy)

> Hi [Name], glad the site's working well! If you know any other business owners who
> could use a similar website, I run a referral offer — £50 for you for every
> referral that becomes a client, and 10% off for them. No pressure, just wanted you
> to know it's there if anyone comes to mind.

## Why this first

Referral + testimonial from real clients converts far better than cold outreach or
ads, costs nothing, and directly feeds the social posts in
[posts-portfolio-spotlight.md](posts-portfolio-spotlight.md) (adding a real quote to
each one). Do this before spending on ads.
