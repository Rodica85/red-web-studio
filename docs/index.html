FAQ

Frequently
Asked Questions.

Who owns the website after delivery?+

You do. 100%. You own the code, content, domain, and all design assets. Unlike Wix or Squarespace, we don't lock you in. If you ever want to move providers, you take everything with you.

What if I'm not happy with the design?+

We offer unlimited revisions until you're happy. Most projects need 1-2 rounds of tweaks. If after that you still want out, we offer a 14-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked.

How much does hosting cost per year?+

Hosting typically costs £60-£120/year depending on traffic. We set you up on a reputable UK host (or one you choose). You pay them directly. No markup, no recurring fees from RED unless you want ongoing support.

Is the AI chatbot GDPR compliant? Where is data stored?+

Yes. We use UK/EU-based AI providers and store all conversation data in compliance with GDPR. We provide a privacy policy template covering chatbot interactions, and we never train models on your customer data.

Can I edit the content myself after launch?+

Yes, every site comes with a simple content editor. We give you a 30-minute walkthrough video and a written guide. Edit text, swap images, add blog posts, all without writing code.

Do you do local SEO?+

Every site includes on-page SEO and local SEO setup: Google Business Profile optimisation, local schema markup, and keyword research targeted to your service area. Most clients rank on page 1 within 3-6 months.

How long does the whole process take?+

Most projects launch in 5-10 days from kickoff. One-page sites: ~5 days. Multi-page sites with AI chatbot: ~7 days. E-commerce builds: ~10 days. The clock starts after the kickoff call and once you've sent us content.

What happens if my AI chatbot gives wrong info?+

We train it on YOUR business: services, FAQs, opening hours, policies. For anything outside its knowledge, it politely says "let me get a human to follow up" and captures the lead. You can review and improve its answers anytime.
